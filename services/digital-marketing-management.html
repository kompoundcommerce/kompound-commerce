Digital Marketing Management

Most brands run paid media through three or four separate vendors — a Google Ads freelancer, a Meta agency, a TikTok creator manager, a marketplace team. Nobody sees the full funnel. Budgets overlap. Attribution breaks. Kompound Commerce runs every paid channel under one roof, with one media plan, one creative pipeline, and one set of numbers the CFO can trust.

Google Ads

Google is still the highest-intent channel in the stack. We build campaigns around incremental conversions — not branded search bloat or Performance Max vanity metrics.

  • Search & Shopping: Query mining, SKAG vs. themed ad groups, negative keyword hygiene, and shopping feed optimization
  • Performance Max: Asset group architecture, exclusions, audience signals, and brand vs. non-brand separation
  • YouTube & Demand Gen: Creative briefs, in-stream placement strategy, and audience build from first-party data
  • Feed management: Merchant Center diagnostics, title/attribute optimization, and supplemental feeds
  • Measurement: Enhanced conversions, GA4 event taxonomy, server-side tagging, and offline conversion imports

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Meta is a creative testing game. Winning accounts ship dozens of new assets every week and let the algorithm find the winners. We run the creative engine that makes that possible.

  • Advantage+ Shopping campaigns: Consolidated structures, creative diversity, and existing-customer budget caps
  • Creative testing framework: Static vs. video, UGC vs. polished, hook variations, and format-specific edits for Reels, Stories, and feed
  • Catalog & retargeting: Dynamic product ads, DABA, and warm audience segmentation
  • Conversions API: Server-side CAPI, deduplication, and attribution windows that actually reflect reality
  • Creator partnerships: Partnership Ads, whitelisting, and branded content rights management

TikTok Ads

TikTok rewards native content at a pace no other platform does. You cannot repurpose a Meta ad and hope it works. We build TikTok-first creative systems — often with creators already in your orbit.

  • Spark Ads: Whitelisted creator content as the core of your ad strategy
  • Video Shopping Ads: Catalog-driven shoppable video campaigns
  • Smart+ campaigns: TikTok's answer to Advantage+, with proper creative diversity
  • Creator sourcing: Affiliate and paid creator pipelines, brief templates, and UGC rights
  • TikTok Shop coordination: Tight loop between TikTok Ads Manager and TikTok Shop for unified attribution

YouTube Ads

YouTube sits inside Google Ads but deserves a dedicated creative and measurement strategy. We run it as a full-funnel channel — not a remnant placement for leftover spend.

  • In-stream, Shorts, and Demand Gen video placements
  • Hook testing, length optimization, and thumbnail A/B
  • Brand lift and view-through conversion measurement
  • Content repurposing from TikTok and Reels creative
