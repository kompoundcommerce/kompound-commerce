Is TikTok Shop Right for Your Brand?

TikTok Shop works best for products that are visual, demonstrable, and carry a story. Apparel, accessories, home goods, beauty, and lifestyle products tend to perform especially well. If your product is better shown than described, TikTok Shop belongs in your channel strategy.
