About

Why Kompound Commerce

Most marketplace agencies were built for one platform — Amazon — and stretched their model to cover the rest. Kompound Commerce was built differently. We started with the belief that modern brands need a partner who can manage their full marketplace footprint as one integrated strategy, not four separate engagements. We operate across Amazon, Walmart, Target Plus, and TikTok Shop — and we treat them as a connected ecosystem, not isolated channels. Because your customer doesn't think in platforms. They discover you on TikTok, buy you on Amazon, and reorder on Walmart. Your agency should see the same picture they do.

What Sets Us Apart

Channel-native expertise

Every platform has a different algorithm, different ad product, and different compliance standard. We don't port Amazon knowledge to Walmart and call it done. We have platform-specific playbooks built from the ground up for each channel we manage.

Full-funnel ownership

We don't hand you a list of recommendations and leave. Kompound Commerce takes operational ownership of your marketplace presence — listings, advertising, inventory, customer feedback, and reporting — so your internal team can focus on building the brand.

Transparent reporting

Every client gets weekly performance reports in plain language. No dashboards you can't interpret. No metrics designed to make us look good. Revenue, ad spend, ACoS, units sold, and what we're doing next — that's your standing weekly update.

Direct communication

You work directly with the people managing your account. No account managers reading from reports your actual team prepared. The person on your weekly call is the person making decisions on your account.

Kompound Commerce exists to help brands grow on marketplaces — without the confusion, the excuses, or the black box.

Our Approach

Every new client relationship begins with a full marketplace audit. You know exactly what we're doing and why from week one.
