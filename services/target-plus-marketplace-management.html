Target Plus Marketplace Management

Target Plus is an invite-only marketplace — and that exclusivity is exactly what makes it valuable. With a curated seller base and Target's 165+ million monthly shoppers, brands approved for Target Plus gain access to a high-intent audience that trusts the Target name. Getting in is hard. Operating it well once you're in is a different challenge entirely. Kompound Commerce manages both sides.

What We Manage

Onboarding & Partner Setup

  • Target Plus application strategy and submission support
  • Partner.io setup and catalog feed configuration
  • Content requirements compliance with Target's strict brand standards
  • Item taxonomy mapping and attribute completion

Listing & Content

  • Product content optimized for Target's search and browse experience
  • Image and lifestyle photography requirements and briefing
  • Product description and feature copy written to Target's style guide
  • Ongoing content audits against Target's quality score benchmarks

Operations

  • Order routing and fulfillment monitoring
  • Seller performance metrics and on-time fulfillment rate protection
  • Return analysis and customer satisfaction tracking
  • Promotional event planning aligned to Target's seasonal calendar

Target Plus isn't just another channel. It's a brand builder. The right products in front of the right customers at scale.

Who Should Consider Target Plus

Target Plus is the right channel for brands with:

  • Clean, well-designed products that fit Target's aesthetic and customer base
  • Reliable fulfillment operations that can meet Target's performance standards
  • A brand identity that benefits from association with Target's positioning
  • Margins that can support Target's pricing structure and promotional calendar

Not sure if you qualify? Kompound Commerce will assess your catalog and tell you honestly whether Target Plus is the right move — and what it would take to get there.
