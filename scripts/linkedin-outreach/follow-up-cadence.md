# Follow-Up Cadence — Multi-Touch Outreach Sequence

## The 5-Touch Framework

Most sellers won't respond to the first message. The money is in the follow-up. Here's a systematic sequence that converts without being annoying.

---

## Day 0 — Connection Request
- Send personalized connection request (use templates from `message-templates.md`)
- Log in `outreach-tracker.csv` with status "Requested"

## Day 1–2 — Accept + First Message
- **If accepted**: Send first follow-up message within 24 hours (value-first, no pitch)
- **If not accepted**: Do nothing yet — let it sit

## Day 3–4 — Engage With Their Content
- Like 1–2 of their recent posts
- Leave a thoughtful comment on something they posted
- This puts you on their radar without being pushy

## Day 5–7 — Second Message (if no reply to first)
- Send a different value angle:
  - Share a relevant blog post from kompoundcommerce.com
  - Share the FBA calculator link
  - Mention a relevant industry update
- Keep it short (3–4 sentences max)

```
Hey {FirstName}, one more thing — thought you might find this useful:

{Blog post link or insight}

Helped a few sellers I know. Let me know if you have questions!
```

## Day 10–14 — Third Touch (The Quick Win)
- Offer something specific and personalized:
  - "I looked at your listing for {product} — noticed your title could rank better for {keyword}"
  - "Your main image could probably convert higher with {suggestion}"
  - "Looks like you might be overpaying on FBA fees for {product}"

```
Hey {FirstName}, I was browsing Amazon and came across your {product}.

Quick observation — {one specific, actionable insight}. That alone could bump your conversion rate.

Happy to share more detail if you're curious. No charge — just like geeking out on listings. 😄
```

## Day 21–28 — Fourth Touch (Social Proof)
- Share a result or case study

```
Hey {FirstName}, quick one — we just helped a {similar_category} seller go from {X ACOS} to {Y ACOS} in 45 days.

Wrote up a quick summary if you want to see what we did: {link or brief breakdown}

Thought of you since you're in a similar space. Hope business is going well! 🚀
```

## Day 45–60 — Fifth Touch (Last Attempt / Re-engage)
- Casual check-in, no pressure

```
Hey {FirstName}, been a minute — hope the Amazon business is crushing it.

We're doing free 15-minute account audits this month for sellers doing $10K+/month. Just a quick screen share where I'll point out any low-hanging fruit.

No sales pitch, just an honest look. Want me to save you a spot?
```

---

## After That

- If no response after 5 touches → move to "Nurture" list
- Nurture = engage with their content occasionally (like/comment once per week)
- Revisit in 90 days with a fresh angle or new resource

---

## Response Handling

### If They Say "Not Interested"
```
Totally understand, {FirstName}. No worries at all — the offer stands if things ever change.

Wishing you the best with your Amazon business! 🙌
```
**Action**: Mark as "Declined" — never message again about services. You can still engage with their content.

### If They Say "Not Right Now"
```
No problem at all! Timing is everything. I'll check back in a couple months — in the meantime, feel free to reach out anytime if something comes up. Good luck with everything! 🤙
```
**Action**: Mark as "Follow Up Later" — set reminder for 60–90 days.

### If They Say "How Much?"
```
Great question! It really depends on where your business is and what you need.

Our services start at around $X/month for {basic_package}, but honestly the best first step is a quick 15-minute call where I can look at your account and tell you exactly what would make the biggest impact.

That way I can give you an accurate quote based on your actual situation. Want to pick a time?
```

### If They Want a Call
```
Awesome, let's do it! Here's my calendar: {calendar_link}

Pick any time that works. It'll be a casual 15-30 min screen share — I'll pull up your listings and give you some honest feedback.

Looking forward to it, {FirstName}! 🤝
```

## Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Connection request acceptance rate | 30–40% |
| Response rate (after first message) | 15–25% |
| Call booking rate (of respondents) | 20–30% |
| Close rate (of calls) | 15–25% |

If your acceptance rate is below 25%, improve your connection request notes.
If your response rate is below 10%, improve your first message (more personalization, more value).
