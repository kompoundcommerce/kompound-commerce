# LinkedIn Content Hooks — Attract Inbound Seller Leads

Post these types of content 3–5x per week on your LinkedIn profile to attract Amazon sellers organically. Every post should make sellers think: "This person knows their stuff."

---

## Post Formats That Work

### 1. Quick Win Posts (highest engagement)
Short, actionable tips sellers can use immediately.

**Example:**
```
Most Amazon sellers are wasting money on PPC because of one setting.

Go to Campaign Manager → Placement Adjustments → check "Top of Search."

If you're not bidding 30-50% higher for Top of Search, you're giving those
high-converting spots to your competitors.

Quick fix. Big impact.

#AmazonFBA #AmazonPPC #AmazonSeller
```

### 2. Myth-Busting Posts
Challenge common seller misconceptions.

**Example:**
```
"You need 50+ reviews to rank on Amazon."

No, you don't. Here's what actually matters:

→ Keyword relevance in your listing (title, bullets, backend)
→ Click-through rate (main image + price)
→ Conversion rate (A+ content, images, reviews quality)
→ Sales velocity (PPC driving initial momentum)

I've seen listings with 12 reviews outrank listings with 500.

Reviews help, but they're not the whole game.

#AmazonSeller #AmazonFBA #Ecommerce
```

### 3. Before/After Case Studies
Show results without naming the client (unless permitted).

**Example:**
```
Amazon PPC results for a supplements seller we work with:

Before (January):
→ ACOS: 52%
→ Ad spend: $8,400/mo
→ Ad revenue: $16,150/mo

After (March):
→ ACOS: 28%
→ Ad spend: $6,200/mo
→ Ad revenue: $22,100/mo

Less spend. More revenue. Lower ACOS.

What changed? Three things:
1. Killed zombie keywords eating budget
2. Moved winners to exact match campaigns
3. Added Sponsored Brand Video (most sellers ignore this)

If your ACOS is over 40%, there's almost certainly money being wasted.

#AmazonPPC #AmazonFBA #AmazonSeller
```

### 4. "What I'd Do If..." Posts
Hypothetical scenarios that demonstrate expertise.

**Example:**
```
If I had to launch a new Amazon product in 2026 with $5,000, here's exactly what I'd do:

Week 1-2:
→ Product photography: $800 (7 images + infographics)
→ A+ Content design: $400
→ Keyword research: $0 (use free tools)

Week 3-4:
→ PPC launch campaign: $1,500 budget
→ 3 campaign types: Auto, Exact, Broad
→ Bid aggressively on top 5 keywords only

Week 5-8:
→ Remaining $2,300 into PPC
→ Optimize weekly: cut losers, scale winners
→ Run Vine for first 30 reviews

Day 60 target: 30+ reviews, ranking page 1 for 3+ keywords.

Forget the gurus saying you need $50K. You need strategy.

#AmazonFBA #ProductLaunch #Ecommerce
```

### 5. Amazon News/Update Commentary
Break down Amazon announcements and what they mean for sellers.

**Example:**
```
Amazon just updated their FBA fee structure for Q3 2026.

Here's what actually matters for small sellers:

✅ Good news: Inbound placement fee reduced 15%
⚠️ Watch out: Low-inventory-level fee threshold increased
❌ Bad news: Referral fees up 0.5% in 3 categories

What to do right now:
1. Recalculate your margins with the new fees
2. Increase safety stock if you're borderline on inventory levels
3. Check if your category is affected by referral fee changes

Free FBA calculator to run your new numbers: [link]

#AmazonFBA #AmazonSeller #Ecommerce
```

### 6. Poll/Question Posts
Drive engagement and identify prospects in comments.

**Example:**
```
Amazon sellers — what's your biggest challenge right now?

🔴 PPC costs too high
🟡 Can't rank organically
🟢 Expanding to Walmart
🔵 Inventory/supply chain

Drop a comment with details — I'll try to help.

#AmazonFBA #AmazonSeller
```

**Why this works**: People who answer = self-identified prospects with specific problems.
