# LinkedIn Search Strategies — Finding Small Amazon Sellers

## 1. Direct Title Searches

Use LinkedIn's search bar with these job titles (wrap in quotes for exact match):

```
"Amazon Seller"
"Amazon FBA"
"FBA Seller"
"Amazon FBA Seller"
"Amazon Private Label"
"E-commerce Seller"
"Amazon Store Owner"
"Amazon Brand Owner"
"Founder" + Amazon
"Owner" + Amazon FBA
"CEO" + Amazon
"Ecommerce Entrepreneur"
"Amazon Wholesale"
```

### Filters to Apply
- **Connections**: 2nd and 3rd degree (not already connected)
- **Locations**: United States, Canada, United Kingdom (English-speaking markets)
- **Company size**: 1–10 employees, 11–50 employees (small sellers)
- **Industry**: Retail, Consumer Goods, E-Commerce, Wholesale

---

## 2. LinkedIn Group Mining

Join these groups to find concentrated seller audiences:

### High-Value Groups
- Amazon FBA Sellers
- Amazon Private Label Sellers
- Amazon FBA & Private Label Mastermind
- E-Commerce Entrepreneurs
- Amazon Sellers Network
- Walmart Marketplace Sellers
- Amazon PPC & Advertising
- Amazon Brand Registry & IP

### How to Use Groups
1. Join 5–8 relevant groups
2. Browse member lists — these are self-identified sellers
3. Post helpful content (not pitches) to build visibility
4. Connect with active members who post questions or problems
5. Members who ask about PPC, listings, or scaling = warm prospects

---

## 3. Boolean Search Strings

Use in LinkedIn's search bar or Sales Navigator:

### Find FBA Sellers
```
("Amazon FBA" OR "private label" OR "Amazon seller") AND ("founder" OR "owner" OR "CEO") NOT "agency" NOT "consultant" NOT "coach"
```

### Find Struggling/New Sellers
```
("Amazon seller" OR "FBA") AND ("just launched" OR "new to" OR "learning" OR "starting")
```

### Find Sellers Ready to Scale
```
("Amazon FBA" OR "Amazon brand") AND ("scaling" OR "growing" OR "6 figure" OR "7 figure") AND ("founder" OR "owner")
```

### Find Walmart-Curious Amazon Sellers
```
("Amazon seller" OR "Amazon FBA") AND ("Walmart" OR "multichannel" OR "expand")
```

## 5. Competitor Follower Poaching

Find followers/connections of these types of accounts:

- Amazon seller coaches and course creators
- Amazon tools (Helium 10, Jungle Scout, Sellics)
- Other Amazon agencies (their followers are your prospects)
- Amazon-focused podcasts and YouTube channels

**How**: View their posts → see who likes/comments → those are active sellers.

## 7. Ideal Prospect Signals

Prioritize prospects who show these signs:

### Hot Prospects (reach out immediately)
- [ ] Posts about struggling with PPC costs or ACOS
- [ ] Mentions losing Buy Box or listing suppression
- [ ] Asks questions about Walmart expansion
- [ ] Complains about Amazon fees eating margins
- [ ] Recently posted about account health issues

### Warm Prospects (good candidates)
- [ ] Running 1–3 products on Amazon
- [ ] Solo founder or tiny team (1–5 people)
- [ ] Revenue between $10K–$500K/month (small but active)
- [ ] Has been selling 6–24 months (past the honeymoon phase)
- [ ] Active in Amazon seller groups

### Cold Prospects (lower priority)
- [ ] Just "interested" in Amazon selling, hasn't started
- [ ] Large brand with in-house team already
- [ ] Already working with another agency
