Automating Walmart Connect Ads with AI Bidding Strategies

Walmart Connect is where Amazon PPC was 5 years ago — early, less competitive, and full of opportunity. AI bidding gives you the edge to capitalize before it gets crowded.

Why Walmart Connect Is a Massive Opportunity

Walmart Connect — Walmart's advertising platform — is growing at 30%+ year-over-year. But advertiser adoption is still a fraction of Amazon's. This means lower CPCs, less competition, and better ROAS for early adopters. The sellers investing in Walmart ads now are building the same compounding advantage that early Amazon advertisers enjoyed in 2018-2020.

Walmart Connect Ad Types

Sponsored Products

Keyword-targeted ads that appear in search results and product pages. The bread and butter of Walmart advertising. Supports automatic and manual targeting with broad, phrase, and exact match types. CPCs typically run 30-50% lower than equivalent Amazon Sponsored Products.

Sponsored Brands

Banner-style ads featuring your brand logo, headline, and multiple products. Appear at the top of search results. Great for brand awareness and driving traffic to your Walmart Brand Shop. Limited availability — requires Walmart approval.

Display Ads

Programmatic display ads across Walmart's properties and partner sites. Support retargeting, audience targeting, and category targeting. Minimum budgets apply — typically $1,000+/month.

The CPC advantage: Average Walmart Sponsored Products CPC is $0.30-0.80 for most categories, compared to $0.80-2.50+ on Amazon. This means you can test keywords and campaigns at a fraction of the cost. The brands investing now are building data and ranking advantages that will compound as competition increases.

How AI Bidding Works on Walmart

Automated Keyword Discovery

Start with automatic campaigns and let AI harvest converting search terms. Walmart's auto-targeting algorithm is less sophisticated than Amazon's, so manual refinement is more important. AI tools accelerate the harvesting process by analyzing search term reports daily instead of weekly.

Bid Optimization

AI tools analyze conversion patterns by keyword, time of day, and day of week — then adjust bids accordingly. Since Walmart has less historical data than Amazon, AI models need more time to learn. Expect 30-60 days for the algorithm to fully optimize.

Budget Pacing

Walmart campaigns can burn through daily budgets quickly if not managed. AI pacing distributes spend evenly across the day, preventing morning burn-outs that miss evening shopping peaks.

Walmart Connect Campaign Structure

We recommend a modified version of our Amazon 4-tier architecture:

  1. Brand Defense (15% of budget): Exact match on brand terms. Protect your brand traffic from competitors.
  2. Core Performers (45% of budget): Exact and phrase match on proven converting keywords. Optimize weekly based on 14-day data windows.
  3. Discovery (30% of budget): Automatic campaigns and broad match to find new keywords. Harvest winners weekly and promote to Core Performers.
  4. Expansion (10% of budget): Category targeting and competitor conquesting. Higher ACoS accepted for market share growth.

Key Differences from Amazon PPC

  • Longer attribution window: Walmart uses a 14-day click attribution window (Amazon uses 7 days by default). This means campaigns may look more profitable on Walmart.
  • Less granular data: Walmart's reporting is less detailed than Amazon's. AI tools help fill gaps with modeled data.
  • Slower optimization cycles: Less traffic means longer time to statistical significance. Patience is required — don't make bid changes based on 3 days of data.
  • Placement controls: Walmart offers fewer placement bid adjustments than Amazon. Focus on keyword-level optimization instead.

Measuring Walmart Connect ROAS

  • Target ROAS: Aim for 4-6x ROAS on Walmart (equivalent to 15-25% ACoS). Lower competition makes this achievable.
  • Organic halo effect: Walmart advertising lifts organic ranking more transparently than Amazon. Track total sales (organic + ad) against ad spend for true ROI.
  • Cross-marketplace impact: Walmart presence can strengthen your overall brand, driving Amazon customers to your Walmart listing and vice versa.
